What we collect

D-REC is a platform for registering renewable-energy production facilities and issuing distributed renewable-energy certificates (D-RECs). To do that, we collect:

  • Account information — name, email, organization, role, and (where required) phone number, used to authenticate you and route notifications.
  • Facility documentation — the documents you upload for each device or site, including Form SF-02 / SF-02C, single-line diagrams (SLDs), commissioning / COD proofs, ownership declarations, metering evidence, project photos, and any supporting attachments.
  • Production data — meter readings and time-series generation data submitted via API or manual upload.
  • Usage data — basic technical logs (timestamps, IP address, browser, error traces) needed to operate and secure the service.

How we use it

  • To register, verify, and audit your facilities.
  • To issue and account for D-REC certificates.
  • To make data available to authorised reviewers, registrants, and buyers who participate in the same workflow.
  • To operate, debug, and secure the platform.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

AI-assisted auto-fill

Some platform features use AI to read documents you upload and pre-fill fields (site name, capacity, commissioning date, etc.) to save reviewer and registrant time. To do this, we send the content of the relevant document (e.g. an SLD, SF-02, or COD proof) to Anthropic, the provider of the Claude language model used by the extractor.

Anthropic processes that content solely to return the extracted fields to us. Under Anthropic's terms, the content is not used to train Anthropic's models, and is retained only briefly (typically up to 30 days) for trust and safety review before being deleted. Where required, we have a Data Processing Agreement in place with Anthropic.

AI-assisted auto-fill suggestions are reviewed by a human before they are written to the official record. You can also upload your documents and fill the fields manually instead.

Who can see what you upload

  • Your own organisation sees the devices and documents you create.
  • D-REC reviewers see the devices and supporting documents they are asked to review, in order to verify them.
  • Buyers see only the registry-level information needed to evaluate and retire certificates (project name, country, capacity, generation totals); they do not get access to your raw source documents.
  • D-REC operations and engineering staff may access data as needed to operate, debug, or audit the platform.

Sub-processors

We use a small number of trusted vendors to run the service:

  • Amazon Web Services (AWS) — hosting, object storage, and backups.
  • Anthropic — AI document extraction (auto-fill), as described above.
  • Transactional email provider — to send sign-in and notification emails.

Each sub-processor is bound by confidentiality and data-protection terms appropriate to its role.

Retention

Account and facility records are retained for as long as your account is active and for a reasonable period after closure to comply with audit, legal, and certificate-traceability requirements. You may request deletion of personal data by contacting us at the address below; some records may need to be retained in pseudonymised form to preserve the integrity of already-issued certificates.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights — or to ask any question about this policy — write to privacy@drec.energy.

Changes

We will update this page when the way we handle data changes materially. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent change.
